IncomeFlow questions

Your payments stay on the account you sign in with.

Who can see my income?

Only you, after you sign in.

Which currencies?

Czech koruna, euro, and US dollar. The month shows the one you pick.

How does a split work?

One payment is divided across the months you choose. An equal split puts any leftover cents on the last month.

What if the months do not add up?

The payment is not saved until the months match the total.

Can I remove a payment?

Yes. Removing it deletes that payment and nothing else.
